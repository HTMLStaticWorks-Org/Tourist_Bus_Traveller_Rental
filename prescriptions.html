Rental Pricing Questions

How is garage-to-garage distance calculated?

Odometer readings start and end at our fleet depot. You receive a clear starting and closing reading on your digital trip sheet.

What does the daily driver allowance cover?

The daily allowance covers your chauffeur's meals and overnight rest during outstation trips, so you never have to arrange separate crew accommodations.

How do advance bookings and invoices work?

A small booking advance locks your vehicle and driver. Final balance and itemized PDF invoices are accessible in your Customer Dashboard.
